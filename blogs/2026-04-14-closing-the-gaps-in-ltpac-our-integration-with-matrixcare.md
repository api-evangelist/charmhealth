When it comes to long-term and post-acute care (LTPAC), the most precarious moments often don’t happen in the ICU or the operating room—they happen during the handoff. This is because significant knowledge gaps exist between hospitals, clinicians, payers and other care partners— gaps that can negatively impact patients and their care. It’s a workflow problem of the highest order.
